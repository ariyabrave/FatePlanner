EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE

Plan your life without giving your data away.

FatePlanner combines daily planning, habits, study tools and personal finance in one private desktop application.

✓

Tasks & Planning

Daily and weekly planning, subtasks, recurring tasks and a dedicated task center.

☀

Jalali Calendar

Designed around Persian workflows with Jalali dates and full right-to-left support.

◆

Habits & Streaks

Build routines, track completion and follow your consistency over time.

◷

Study & Focus

Subjects, study sessions, progress tracking and a built-in focus timer.

₮

Personal Finance

Track income, expenses, monthly budgets, categories and savings goals.

↗

Analytics

Understand your productivity, habits, study activity and progress with visual reports.

◈

Backup & Export

Automatic backups, manual backups, restore tools and data export.

◐

Light & Dark Themes

A polished responsive desktop interface with system, light and dark appearance modes.

PRIVACY FIRST

Your planner stays on your computer.

FatePlanner works entirely offline during normal use. There are no accounts, no cloud sync requirement and no telemetry.

✓ Local SQLite database
✓ No registration
✓ No analytics tracking
✓ No required internet connection
